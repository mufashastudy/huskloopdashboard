Break-even Analysis

Explore coating costs, savings, and economic feasibility.

About this view

The coating pays when its cost stays under the break-even line. Break-even coating cost: cmax = P × s / (1 − s). It does not depend on hectares or dose, so one curve serves every field. Inputs follow the Field Advisor tab.
